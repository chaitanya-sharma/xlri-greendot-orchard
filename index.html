Chamber 02Q2 · Which path

Brightwater is the right target. Only a staged deal meets every board target.

We scored seven paths on five criteria. Move the weights to your own view of what matters; the ranking updates live.

A

Seven paths, scored 1 to 5

Weights are yours to change

The Graft Plan stays first under any weighting, because it scores at least as well as every alternative on every criterion. Its weakest score is ease of execution (3 of 5), which is why chamber 05 is about execution.

B

Why not the obvious alternatives

$450M all cash

4.7x trailing revenue for a loss-making brand. Peers sold at about 3-4x. Net debt reaches 2.85x EBITDA and deal NPV turns negative (−$7M).

Partner-to-own

A 25-30% stake with DSD rights and an option. Revenue is not consolidated, so the FY29 targets stay unmet. Kept as our fallback if the seller refuses control.

Distribution only

No control of the brand. KDP carried BodyArmor and lost it to Coca-Cola in 2018; BodyArmor later paid KDP's bottler $350M to settle.

Marrow Lane or Vital alone

Marrow Lane lifts growth to 3.4%. Vital alone needs 24-36 months, and 4 in 10 new grocery items vanish within two years.

So what

Brightwater wins on growth, fit and capability. The deal structure decides whether it also wins on value.

Sources: Case pack; Team Green Dot model v2; KDP 10-K FY2021 (BodyArmor settlement); Bain, insurgent brand growth after acquisition; Marketing Letters (2021), study of 83,719 new grocery items; PepsiCo-PAI juice joint venture (2021).

D

What the board should know

Accounting

We consolidate from Day 1. The founder's 40% is redeemable minority interest, kept outside permanent equity and remeasured each period. Leverage is 2.03x on the lender's definition and 2.36x if we count the founder's right to sell as debt.

Margin

Customer relationships amortise at about $6M a year. FY29 operating margin is 11.5% before that and about 11.3% after, still above 11%.

Approvals

$222M for control is above the $133.9M HSR threshold for 2026. We file at signing, wait 30 days and close around April 2027.

Precedents

KDP bought 60% of GHOST with the rest on a formula. Coca-Cola took a stake in BodyArmor first. Coca-Cola's uncapped fairlife earn-out grew from $270M to $6.2B, so ours is capped.

Chamber 05Q4 · What it takes to execute

Protect what shoppers love. Move the plumbing. Roll out in gated waves.

Poppi's sales fell while it moved onto PepsiCo's trucks. Our rollout is built so that cannot happen all at once.

A

Who owns what after close

Brightwater keeps · Boulder

  • Brand, recipes, design and social voice
  • The 90-day flavour cycle and the 5 AI specialists
  • Natural and specialty stores (65% of sales) on current distributors
  • Its own P&L and a protected marketing budget

Alderbrook runs · the rootstock

  • Canning of core flavours on two can lines
  • Grocery and c-store delivery on DSD

Head of commercial analytics, 2 data engineers, 2 ML engineers, 2 revenue managers and a change lead, plus a champion in each of the 60 DCs. $3M to build, $4.5M a year to run, data and tools included. We buy trend and promotion tools, build the outlet models, and partner for the cloud platform.

C

Safeguards

Deloitte's Trustworthy AI as the checklist: a named owner for each model, a monthly accuracy and drift review, no personal shopper data, and every health claim checked against the fibre dose. Poppi settled a gut-health claims case for $8.9M.

D

Value and realism

Base case +$4.5M FY29 EBITDA after running costs, upside +$9.5M. Only 25% of firms have scaled 40% or more of their AI pilots (Deloitte, 2026), so we start with the two uses that have clear baselines.

So what

Drivers get suggested orders inside the handhelds they already use, with pay tied to accuracy. The data that plans routes today picks the waves that de-risk the deal.

We used NotebookLM and Claude to gather and summarise public sources, to argue against our own plan through a five-person mock judging panel, and to help build this site. A person traced every outside figure here to a filing, a company release or the original report, and we rebuilt every case number in our own model, which also drives the deal simulator in chamber 04.
